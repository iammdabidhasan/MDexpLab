MDexpLab

The official website of MDexpLab, a creative lab for AI, code, UI/UX design, animated video and original sound, run by MD Abid Hasan.

It is a single self-contained file (index.html). There is no build step and no dependencies to install. Open it, edit it, host it.

Live site: https://iammdabidhasan.github.io/MDexpLab/

What is on the site
Section	What it does
Intro	Cinematic loader: traced MD logo, liquid fill, decoding wordmark, curtain split. Plays on the first visit only.
Hero	Full-width animated scene (hex field, circuit traces, terminal, access hex). Has room for a background video.
Services	Auto-scrolling loop of service cards.
Projects	GhostLock and ExoraWorld, with GitHub, website and demo-video links.
Content	YouTube channel card, a big featured video player, an auto-scrolling video carousel, and social links.
About	Animated round profile logo, short bio, skill chips, counters.
Help Center	Contact form (Formspree) and an FAQ accordion.
Footer	Navigation, services, lab links, socials.
Built-in features
Sound, click anywhere. The site starts silent. Click or tap anywhere to turn the generated soundtrack on, click again to turn it off. The M key does the same. Links, buttons, form fields and the video player do not toggle it.
No audio files. The intro, hero and ambient music are all generated live in the browser.
Smart audio. Ambient music ducks while the hero is on screen and while a video pop-up is open, and pauses when the tab is hidden.
Intro only on the first visit. Remembered in the browser. Add ?intro to the address, or use Replay intro in the footer, to see it again.
Responsive. Tested at phone, phone-landscape, tablet and desktop sizes. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.
Extras. Scroll progress bar, back-to-top button, skip-to-content link, marquees that pause when off-screen, spam trap on the contact form, Open Graph and structured-data metadata, favicon.
