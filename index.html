    What is MDexpLab?

    MDexpLab is a creative laboratory where curiosity meets craft. I experiment with AI, write code, design interfaces, compose soundtracks and build things that don’t follow a template.

    This is not just a portfolio. It’s a living lab — a place where ideas get stress-tested, prototyped and sometimes shipped. Every project here started as an experiment.
