    Behind the Lab
    
        
    What is MDexpLab?
    
        
    MDexpLab is a creative laboratory where curiosity meets craft. I experiment with AI, write code, design interfaces, compose soundtracks and build things that don’t follow a template.
    
        
    This is not just a portfolio. It’s a living lab — a place where ideas get stress-tested, prototyped and sometimes shipped. Every project here started as an experiment, and now that includes games: MD Block World is playable free in your browser, with more on the way.
