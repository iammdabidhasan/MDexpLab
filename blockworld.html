HOW TO PLAY

Wander an endless, living world of meadows, sakura groves, lakes and snowy peaks. Build anything, chat with villagers, soar through the sky like a hero, and survive the zombie nights.

TIPS

Zombies come out at night and burn in daylight. Long falls hurt. Sheep, pigs and rabbits roam the meadows. Your world saves automatically.
